Privacy Policy

BreakThree is designed to keep its three-day progress record on your device.

Information stored by the app

BreakThree does not require an account. Progress and app settings are stored locally on the user’s device and, where applicable, shared locally with the BreakThree Home Screen widget through Apple’s App Groups technology.

Data collection

BreakThree does not use advertising, tracking, analytics services, third-party authentication, or external servers to provide its core functionality. The developer does not sell personal information.

Apple services

Downloading BreakThree, receiving updates, using widgets, and optional diagnostic reporting may involve Apple services. Apple handles that information under its own terms, privacy policy, and the settings selected on your device.

External links

If you follow a link from this website or the app to another service, that service handles information according to its own privacy policy.

Changes

This policy may be updated when BreakThree, its data practices, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact tsukigashizumu@gmail.com or visit the BreakThree support page.
