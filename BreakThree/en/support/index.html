Help, contact information, and troubleshooting for BreakThree.

Contact

For questions, bug reports, feedback, or feature requests, contact:

tsukigashizumu@gmail.com

When reporting a problem

Please include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, BreakThree version, the screen where the problem occurred, and a brief description of what happened.

Screenshots or a short screen recording may help us understand the issue.

Basic troubleshooting

Close and reopen BreakThree. If the issue continues, restart the device and confirm that the latest version of the app is installed.

If a Home Screen widget does not update immediately, open BreakThree once, then check the widget again.

Important health information

BreakThree is a personal progress tool. It does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or professional healthcare advice, and it does not guarantee that a user will successfully stop smoking.

If you have health concerns, experience severe symptoms, or plan to use smoking-cessation medication, consult a physician, pharmacist, or other qualified healthcare professional.
