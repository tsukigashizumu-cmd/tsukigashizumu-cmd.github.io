Support

MinPick helps you choose one small action that fits the time you have now.

How to use MinPick

Choose how much time you have, then pick one of the suggestions shown for that amount of time.

Widgets

Add a MinPick widget from the Home Screen widget gallery. If the widget does not update immediately, open MinPick once and try again.

Data and settings

Check MinPick and your device settings if suggestions or widget content do not appear as expected.

Problems after an update

Close and reopen MinPick. If needed, restart the device and make sure the latest version is installed.

Need more help?

When reporting a problem, include your device model, OS version, MinPick version, the screen where the problem occurred, and what you expected to happen.
