Quiet Reserve Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Quiet Reserve is designed as a calm personal reserve tracking app. This page explains how locally saved data, purchases, external services, and contact information are handled.

Basic Policy

Quiet Reserve handles information only within the scope necessary to provide, improve, and support the app.

1. User-entered content and locally saved data

Quiet Reserve may allow users to record reserve amounts, notes, settings, or related information. Data needed for the app's local workflow may be saved on the user's device.

  • Reserve amounts and related notes entered by the user
  • App settings used to restore the app state
  • Information needed to provide app functionality on the device

The developer does not operate a dedicated server to collect the actual reserve records users create in the app. Information handling may be updated depending on the specifications of external services and platforms used by the app.

2. In-app purchases

Quiet Reserve may offer in-app purchases. Payment processing and purchase information management are handled through Apple's systems.

3. Analytics and measurement

Quiet Reserve may use analytics or measurement tools to improve app quality, understand issues, and review usage status.

4. External services

Quiet Reserve may use external services related to in-app purchases, app distribution, crash reports, analytics, iCloud, and app operation. Please refer to each provider's policy for information about how data is handled by external services.

5. Financial disclaimer

Quiet Reserve is a personal tracking utility. It does not provide financial, investment, tax, accounting, or legal advice.

6. Contact

For inquiries about this policy, please contact us using the information listed on the support page.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be reviewed and revised as necessary. If there are important changes, they will be announced in the app or on related pages.
