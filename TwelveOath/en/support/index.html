Support

TwelveOath helps small appointment-based businesses prepare for the moment after a cancellation. This page collects basic help and contact information.

What is TwelveOath for?

It is for recovering empty appointment hours after cancellations. It helps you keep a trusted first circle, match openings, and choose how to announce a slot.

Is it a full booking system?

No. TwelveOath is intentionally focused on cancellation recovery, not every reservation workflow.

Where is my data stored?

The app is designed as a local-first tool. Data you enter is intended to stay on your device unless you choose to share text through another app or your device backup settings copy app data.

Can I use it without a server?

Yes. TwelveOath is designed for independent owners who do not need a large server-based platform.

Contact

For support requests, questions, or bug reports, please contact the developer through the App Store support link or the contact method provided with the app listing.
