PromptDock Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

PromptDock is designed to be used with confidence. This page explains how data is handled. The short version: PromptDock does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Policy Details

Effective date: May 22, 2025  ·  Developer: Tsukigashizumu

1. Information We Collect

PromptDock does not collect any personal information. The app does not require account registration, login, or any form of identity verification.

All data created within the app — including templates, prompt history, saved input sets, field suggestions, and settings — is stored exclusively in your device's local storage (iOS UserDefaults / AppStorage) and never leaves your device.

2. Data Storage

The following data is stored locally on your device:

  • Built-in and custom prompt templates
  • Generated prompt history
  • Saved input sets
  • Field suggestion history
  • Favorite template preferences
  • Tutorial completion status

None of this data is uploaded to any server, cloud service, or third party.

3. Third-Party Services

PromptDock does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or data collection services.

The app includes a button to open ChatGPT (openai.com) in your browser or the ChatGPT app. When you use this feature, your device opens an external URL. Any data you submit to ChatGPT is governed by OpenAI's own privacy policy, which is separate from this policy.

Links to external web pages (this marketing page, Support, Privacy Policy) open in your default browser. No data is passed to these pages.

4. Export & Import

PromptDock allows you to export your custom templates as a .json file using the iOS share sheet. This export is entirely under your control. The developer has no access to exported files.

Importing a .json file reads the file locally on your device. No data is transmitted externally during import.

5. Children's Privacy

PromptDock does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. The app does not collect any personal data from any user, regardless of age.

6. Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes in a material way, the updated version will be published at this URL with a revised effective date. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

7. Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this privacy policy, please reach out via email or through the support page.
