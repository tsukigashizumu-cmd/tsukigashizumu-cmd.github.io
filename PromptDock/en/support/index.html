Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about using PromptDock AI.

How do I create a custom template?

In the Template Picker, scroll down and tap Create Custom Template. Enter a name, system instruction, and up to three input fields. Tap Create Template to save it.

How do I export my custom templates?

Open the Template Picker and tap Export. This opens the iOS share sheet with a .json file you can save to Files, AirDrop to another device, or send via email.

How do I import templates from a JSON file?

Tap Import in the Template Picker and choose a .json file exported from PromptDock AI. Templates with names that already exist will be skipped automatically.

Where is my data stored?

All data — templates, prompt history, saved input sets, and field suggestions — is stored locally on your device using iOS's standard UserDefaults. Nothing is sent to any server.

How do I delete a custom template?

Open the Template Picker, find your custom template, and tap the trash icon on the right side of the card. Built-in templates cannot be deleted.

Can I use PromptDock AI with Claude, Gemini, or other AI tools?

Yes. PromptDock AI generates structured text prompts that work with any AI assistant. The Open ChatGPT button is a shortcut for ChatGPT, but you can copy any prompt and paste it into the tool of your choice.

How do I reset or replay the tutorial?

The tutorial appears automatically on first launch. You can view it again at any time by tapping the ? button in the top-right corner of the app.

Do I need an account?

No. PromptDock AI requires no account, login, or sign-up. Everything works entirely on-device.
