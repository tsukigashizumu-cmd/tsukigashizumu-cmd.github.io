03 / IMPORTANT CONTEXT

It is a notebook, not an automatic stock sensor.

Inventory and usage must be entered and updated by you. The app does not automatically count items, identify objects in photos, place orders, or synchronize inventory to the cloud. Forecasts are estimates based on recorded usage.
